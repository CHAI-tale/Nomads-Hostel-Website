FAQs

What is the franchise model?

Nomads works on a Franchise Owned Franchise Operated (FOFO model). Under this, the franchise owner has to own or lease a property and manage on-ground operations. Nomads helps them in property setup, designing, staff hiring, sales, marketing, and operations.

How much time does it take to start a Nomads Hostel?

Depending upon the scope of work and if you adhere to the timelines set by our onboarding team, it takes roughly 3-4 months from acquisition to final launch. In the case of a greenfield, where the project needs to be built from the ground-up, the duration of construction and final launch takes 6 months to 1 year on average.

How can I convert my property into Nomads Hostel?

Nomads Hostel can be started from an existing constructed property or vacant land. Please fill out the form to tell us more about your property.

Who will look after the design of the property?

Nomads Hostel will be responsible for developing a design of the property. After consultation with the architect-interior, the owner will be responsible for the property’s development under a stipulated timeline.

What is the franchise model?

Nomads works on a Franchise Owned Franchise Operated (FOFO model). Under this, the franchise owner has to own or lease a property and manage on-ground operations. Nomads helps them in property setup, designing, staff hiring, sales, marketing, and operations.

Which location would be best for me to open a Nomads Hostel?

This depends upon your confidence and the feasibility of finding and running the best property in any location. Since only you know your strengths, that is what you need to leverage. You might be from one part of the country and hoping to settle/start a business somewhere entirely different. It is doable, but your challenges will increase.

What is the franchise onboarding process?

Step 1- Fill out the application. Step 2- A one-on-one online discussion with the Nomads team. Step 3- Virtual and physical audit of the selected properties. Step 4- Contract signing and conversion of the property into a Nomads Hostel.
